# Game Bible: neon-vortex

## Identity
- **Name**: neon-vortex
- **Tagline**: Race through a collapsing dimension before reality tears apart
- **What is the player?**: A quantum courier carrying forbidden data through a dying tunnel system
- **What does the world feel like?**: You're inside a vein of light pulsing through the void. The walls breathe. The darkness watches. Every turn could be your last as the tunnel fights to expel you.

## Aesthetics
- **Emotional target**: Adrenaline mixed with dread
- **Color palette**: 
  - Electric cyan (#00FFFF)
  - Hot magenta (#FF00FF)
  - Void black (#000008)
- **Visual reference**: Looks like a neon artery pumping light through absolute darkness, with magenta warning pulses racing ahead
- **Bloom**: Yes, 1.5+ strength

## Music
- **Music identity**: 120bpm, hard synth bass pulse with a screaming lead synth that rises as speed increases. The bass is the heartbeat, the lead is the scream.
- **Music↔gameplay**: BPM starts at 100, increases 10bpm each stage (max 150). New layers at stages 3 and 5. Musical hits on every dodge.

## Stages
1. **Awakening** - slow, sparse obstacles, single instrument
2. **Accelerando** - faster, more obstacles  
3. **Fracture** - new obstacle type + second music layer
4. **Collapse** - very fast, screen warps slightly
5. **Singularity** - maximum speed, chromatic aberration, full band

## The Moment
- Stage 5 transition where everything tries to kill you at once while music reaches climax

## Lore
The Vortex was built to transport quantum data between dimensions. It collapsed when someone tried to send consciousness through it. Now it eats ships and spits out their broken signals.
